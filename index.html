Resume

Projects

  • Time Tracking App

    This is a mobile app that I created for tracking student worker timecards at the Drop-In Support Desk. This app is connected to a database we used to verify the validity of their hours worked. The desk is still using the app to this day to track student worker hours.

  • Poster Printing App

    This is used by the Drop-In Support Desk and CopyCat Print Service to track posters being handed out in the Hillman Library. Once a job is printed CopyCat adds the job to the database using this app and once the poster is delivered to the Drop-In Support Desk the staff marks the poster as delievered. Finally, when the end user picks up the poster the job is marked as completed and database is updated.

  • DateChange App

    Used by the Full Time Employees at the Drop-In Support Desk to easily check previous weeks hours worked. This was a GUI of our database that allowed users to easily fetch the total hours worked that week, the average hours per student worker, and what each shift every student worked.

  • Restraunt Site

    This was the Final Project in CS1530 at the University of Pittsburgh. We needed to create an API that was able to interact with an orders database. User's were able to publish an order to the database and on the backend the kitchen is able to see all the orders and clear them as they are made.

  • Printing Outage Site

    This was a API created for a Software Engineering course at the University of Pittsburgh. We created a way to track what printers were operational on campus along with the reasons they were offline. Their was a student portal along with an admin portal. Students could see what printers were online and mark printers as offline if one wasn't working. Admin's were able to add printer, delete printers, and mark printers as fixed.

  • HELI Lab

    This is a site I helped create as part of my Capstone at the University of Pittsburgh. I was the Scrum Master throughout this project, but heavily assisted in creating uniform code and standardizing the appearence across team members and pages. We relocated the HELI Lab page to a new hosting platform, updated information from the old site, and stylized the page in a more up to date fasion.
